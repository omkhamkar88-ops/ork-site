PRIVACY

Last updated 30 October 2026

ORK is a habit tracker that keeps your habits on your phone. This page says exactly what leaves it, and what never does.

What never leaves your phone

Your habit names, the days you ticked them, your counts, your checklist steps and your notes stay in a database on your device. They are not sent to us, and we have no way to read them.

Your birthday, if you enter one, is stored only on your phone. It is never synced and never shown to anyone.

Your profile photo is stored on your phone and never leaves it. People you share habits with see your name, not your photo.

What we never collect

ORK contains no analytics and no usage tracking. We do not measure which screens you open or how often you use the app.

There is no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracker of any kind. Nothing in ORK reports back about you.

We never ask for your location, contacts, messages or calendar.

What we collect, and why

If you sign in with Google

We receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them to know which account is yours, so that habits you share reach the right person and your backup can be found again.

This is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. Your sign-in records include the date you joined and when you last signed in.

If you use ORK without an account

We collect nothing. Guest mode has no account, no sharing and no backup. Everything stays on your phone.

Your backup

When you are signed in, ORK keeps one encrypted file in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that only ORK can see. We do not hold a copy.

The key that opens that file is stored in your own ORK account. Neither Google Drive nor our servers can read your backup alone. You can turn backup off in Settings at any time.

ORK asks for the narrowest Google Drive permission that exists. It can only see files it created itself. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.

Sharing habits with other people

When you share a habit with someone in Lanes, that habit and its day values are end-to-end encrypted on your phone before they are sent, and decrypted on theirs. Our relay passes the sealed message along without being able to read it.

Only the habits you switch on are shared, per person. Habits you have not shared are never sent anywhere.

Who else is involved

  • Google (Firebase) — sign-in, and passing encrypted messages between people sharing habits.
  • Google Drive — stores your encrypted backup, in your own account.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising. There are no advertising networks in ORK.

How long we keep it

Your account record lasts as long as your account. Encrypted messages between people sharing habits are deleted once delivered, or after 30 days.

Deleting everything

Settings has Delete account and data. It removes your account, anything we hold about you, and your backup, then clears the app on your phone. Habits on your device go with it.

You can also remove ORK's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

ORK is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. In India, this is your right under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before the change takes effect.
