What it is

A habit tracker that keeps your habits on your phone. Tick things off, build streaks, and see months of progress at a glance.

ORK has Lanes: compare yourself with one person at a time, using only the habits you each choose to share. Everything shared is end-to-end encrypted, so nobody in the middle can read it — including us.

How it treats your data

Local-first. Your habits live in a database on your phone. Every screen works offline.

Private by default. Nothing is shared until you switch it on, per habit and per person.

Your backup, your Drive. If you sign in, ORK keeps one encrypted file in your own Google Drive so a new phone picks up where the old one left off. We cannot read it.

We never collect what you track. Habit names, logs and notes never leave your phone unencrypted.

Where it is

ORK is in development, Android first. It is not on the Play Store yet.
